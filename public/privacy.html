Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Controller

The controller responsible for data processing on this website within the meaning of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is:

byereviews
Email: info@byereviews.com

2. Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted by Hostinger International Ltd., 61 Lordou Vironos Street, 6023 Larnaca, Cyprus. When you visit the website, the server automatically records information that your browser transmits (IP address, date and time, requested page, referrer, browser and operating system). This is necessary to deliver the website securely and stably (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Log files are deleted after a short period, unless longer storage is required to investigate security incidents. We have concluded a data processing agreement with our host.

3. Fonts, cookies and web analytics

Fonts, scripts and media of this website are served from our own server; no data is sent to Google Fonts or other font CDNs. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

To understand how our website is used, we use Ahrefs Web Analytics, provided by Ahrefs Pte. Ltd., 16 Raffles Quay, #33-03 Hong Leong Building, Singapore 048581. Ahrefs Web Analytics works without cookies and does not create cross-site user profiles. When you visit a page, your browser loads a script from analytics.ahrefs.com, which records aggregated information such as the page visited, referrer, device type, browser, country and visit duration. Your IP address is transmitted to Ahrefs for technical reasons but is not stored in a way that identifies you. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in analysing and improving our website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Where data is transferred to Singapore, this is based on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. You can object to this processing at any time, for example by blocking the script in your browser. More information: ahrefs.com/legal/privacy-policy.

To see where visitors leave the order form, we record anonymous funnel steps ourselves (visited the order page, started a search, selected a business profile, selected reviews, entered contact details, submitted the order). Each step is stored with a random ID that exists only in your browser tab (session storage, deleted when you close the tab), the traffic source (e.g. search engine or our blog), the country from your browser language and – for the selected business – its public Google profile data. We do not store your IP address, do not use cookies for this and do not combine the data with other sources. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (improving our order form).

Google Ads conversion measurement. We advertise on Google Search (Google Ads, Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). When you reach our website by clicking one of these ads, Google adds a click identifier (gclid) to the link. Our page keeps it only in the memory of the open tab – no cookie is set and nothing is stored in your browser. If you then place an order, the click identifier and the campaign tags of the link (e.g. utm_source, utm_campaign) are stored with your order. We transmit the click identifier together with the time and value of the order and, later, of the payment to Google so we can see which ads lead to orders (offline conversion import). We do not transmit your name, email address or any other contact details. Click identifiers older than 90 days are no longer transmitted. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in measuring the effectiveness of our advertising (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Google may process data in the USA; Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. You can object at any time by emailing info@byereviews.com; we will then not transmit your order to Google. More information: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Customer account and login cookie

When you place your first order we create a customer account with your email address as the username and send you a password by email. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash. When you log in, we set a single session cookie (br_session) that keeps you logged in until you close your browser. It is technically necessary for the dashboard and is not used for tracking (§ 25(2) TDDDG, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

5. Finding your business and reviews

When you search for your business in the order form, the search term is sent to the Google Places API (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) to find the matching Google Business Profile, and the publicly visible reviews of that profile are retrieved via Google and via SerpApi, LLC (5540 N Lamar Blvd #12, Austin, TX 78756, USA). Only the search term and the ID of the business profile are transmitted – no personal data about you. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (preparing your order).

6. Payment

Payments are processed by Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd. (1 Grand Canal Street Lower, Dublin 2, Ireland). When you click “Pay”, you are redirected to Stripe, which processes your payment details under its own privacy policy; we only receive the payment status and your email address. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

7. Orders

When you place an order we process the data you enter (name, email address, company, phone number, address, country, the business profile and the reviews you submit) in order to perform the contract, communicate with you and issue invoices (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and to comply with statutory retention obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR). To request removal of a review, we pass the necessary information (e.g. the review link and the reason for removal) to Google. We store order data for as long as necessary for these purposes and in accordance with statutory retention periods (tax and commercial law, generally up to 7–10 years).

8. Contact by email

If you contact us by email, we process your message and contact data to handle your enquiry (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). We delete the data once your enquiry has been dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 15–21 GDPR). You can withdraw any consent given at any time with effect for the future. To exercise your rights, write to info@byereviews.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

10. Security

This website uses TLS encryption (recognisable by “https://” in the address bar) to protect the data you transmit.
