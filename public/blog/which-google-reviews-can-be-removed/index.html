Which Google Reviews Can Be Removed? Every Policy Violation Explained

M Maximilian · Founder, byereviews ·Last updated Sep 30, 2026·8 min read
SHORT ANSWER

Google removes reviews that break its user-contributed content policy – not reviews that are negative, harsh or unfair. The main categories are fake engagement, conflict of interest, off-topic content, harassment and hate speech, personal information, offensive or sexual content, and illegal content.

The policy violations, one by one

ViolationWhat it meansTypical example
Fake engagementNot based on a real experience, paid, incentivised or posted by bots“Never been here, heard it's awful”
Conflict of interestPosted by competitors, current or former employees, or the ownerA rival shop owner's 1-star review
Off-topicNot about the experience at this placePolitical rants, complaints about a different business
Harassment & hate speechAttacks on individuals or groupsInsults aimed at a named employee
Personal informationPhone numbers, addresses, private detailsA staff member's home address
Offensive & sexual contentProfanity, obscene or sexually explicit contentSlurs, explicit language
Advertising & spamLinks, promotions, repeated content“Visit my channel for real reviews”
ImpersonationPretending to be someone elseA review posted in a real customer's name
Illegal or dangerous contentContent that breaks the law or promotes harmThreats

What doesn't count as a violation

  • A genuine 1-star review that's unfair or exaggerated.
  • Criticism of prices, waiting times or service.
  • A review you disagree with factually – unless it's defamatory, which is a legal matter: see defamatory reviews.
  • Star-only ratings without text – there's nothing for Google to assess.

How to use this list

When you report a review, pick the one violation that fits best and be ready to explain why. For fake reviews, follow our guide to proving they're fake; for competitors and ex-employees, see conflict-of-interest reviews. If Google rejects the report, the appeal is your second chance.

FAQ

Can a review be removed just because it's unfair?

No. Unfair isn't a policy violation. It has to break one of the rules above.

What about reviews in another language?

Language alone isn't a reason. The content still has to break a policy.

Can old reviews be removed?

Yes. There's no time limit on policy violations, though older reviews usually need more evidence.
