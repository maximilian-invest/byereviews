How to Remove a Google Review From a Competitor or Former Employee

M Maximilian · Founder, byereviews ·Last updated Sep 30, 2026·7 min read
SHORT ANSWER

Reviews from competitors, current or former employees violate Google's conflict-of-interest policy and can be removed. The challenge is showing the connection – so collect anything that links the reviewer to a rival business or your former staff before you report.

Why these reviews break the rules

Google doesn't allow reviews from people with a stake in the business – positive or negative. A competitor or an ex-employee with a grudge isn't reviewing a customer experience, which makes it a conflict of interest.

How to spot them

  • The reviewer's name matches a former employee, or a nickname they used.
  • Inside details only staff would know: rotas, internal disputes, names of colleagues.
  • The account reviewed a competitor positively around the same time.
  • A spike of 1-star reviews right after someone left or a rival opened nearby.

Step by step

  1. Document the connection: employment dates, the competitor link, matching timelines.
  2. Report the review under conflict of interest.
  3. Track it in the Reviews Management Tool.
  4. If rejected, appeal with the documentation – see the appeal guide.

When it's also fake

Competitors often post from accounts that never visited you. In that case the review is also fake engagement – report under whichever violation you can support best. The full list is in which reviews can be removed.

FAQ

Can I name the former employee in my reply?

Don't. Naming people publicly can create legal trouble and breaks Google's personal information rules.

What if the ex-employee's review is true?

It's still a conflict of interest under Google's policy, but expect Google to look at it carefully. Keep your evidence factual.
