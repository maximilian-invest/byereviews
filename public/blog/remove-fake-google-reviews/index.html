How to Remove Fake Google Reviews (and Prove They're Fake)

M Maximilian · Founder, byereviews ·Last updated Sep 30, 2026·9 min read
SHORT ANSWER

Fake Google reviews violate Google's fake engagement policy and can be removed – but Google wants a reason to believe the review isn't genuine. Check your records, look for patterns across the reviewer's profile, report under “fake engagement” and keep the evidence ready for the appeal.

Signs a review is fake

  • No record of the customer: no booking, invoice, table reservation or call.
  • The reviewer profile is new, has no photo and only one or two reviews.
  • Several reviews in a short window with similar wording.
  • Details that don't match your business: services you don't offer, wrong location, staff who never worked there.
  • The same account reviewed your direct competitors positively on the same day.

Step by step: removing a fake review

  1. Search your records for the reviewer's name, date and details.
  2. Open the reviewer's profile and screenshot their review history.
  3. Report the review in your Business Profile and choose the fake engagement option.
  4. Track the status in the Reviews Management Tool.
  5. If it's rejected, file the appeal with your evidence – see how to win the one-time appeal.

How to prove it

You don't need courtroom proof, but you need more than “this person was never here”. The strongest evidence:

  • A statement that no customer by that name or at that time exists in your system.
  • Screenshots showing the account's pattern (new account, many 1-star reviews, reviews in other cities on the same day).
  • Timeline of several reviews appearing together – see review bombing if it's a wave.
  • Any link to a competitor or former employee – see conflict-of-interest reviews.

What if it's not clearly fake?

If a real customer left an exaggerated but genuine review, it probably won't qualify as fake. Check the other policy violations – harassment, off-topic content or personal information can still get it removed.

FAQ

Can Google see who wrote a fake review?

Google has signals you don't – devices, locations, account history – which is why a well-documented report often succeeds even if you can't identify the person.

Should I reply to a fake review?

A short, calm reply like “We have no record of your visit, please contact us” is fine and helps readers. Don't accuse or argue.

How many fake reviews can I report at once?

As many as there are. Report each one individually and track them in the Reviews Management Tool.
