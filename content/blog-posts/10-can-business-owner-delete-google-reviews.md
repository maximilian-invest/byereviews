# Can a Business Owner Delete Google Reviews? The Honest Answer

**No. A business owner can't delete Google reviews – not even on their own profile. You can reply to them, and you can report reviews that break Google's policies so Google removes them. Anyone who says you can simply delete reviews is wrong or selling something.**

## What owners can do

- Reply publicly to any review.
- Report reviews that break Google's policies – see [which ones qualify](/blog/which-google-reviews-can-be-removed).
- Track reports and appeal a rejection – see [the appeal guide](/blog/google-review-appeal).
- Ask happy customers to leave honest reviews (never pay or incentivise them).

## What owners can't do

- Delete or hide reviews.
- Turn off reviews on a Business Profile.
- Remove a review by deleting and recreating the profile – reviews are tied to the place.
- Pay for positive reviews – illegal under the FTC rule in the US and the DMCC Act in the UK.

## The fastest honest route

If a review is [fake](/blog/remove-fake-google-reviews) or clearly breaks a rule, report it today. The full process is in [how to remove a Google review](/blog/how-to-remove-a-google-review).

## FAQ

### Can I delete a review I left as a customer?

Yes – reviewers can delete their own reviews. Owners can't delete other people's.

### Does replying help?

Yes. A calm reply shows future customers how you handle problems, and it doesn't affect removal.
